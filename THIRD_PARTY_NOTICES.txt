ApeRadar Next is a TypeScript implementation informed by offline analysis of
ApeRadar 2.1.0. The initial ship catalog is from the original MIT-licensed
ApeRadar distribution. Copyright (c) 2023 zylalx1; see LICENSE. Current ship
details and localized names are refreshed through the Wargaming Public API;
legacy entries absent from the API are retained. Game data and related names
belong to their respective owners and are not covered by this project's MIT
license. See docs/ship-catalog.md for the update process and API references.

Original Chinese ship-name restoration is informed by the workflow in
https://github.com/jxzhangaoran/aperadar_shiplist_update . The TypeScript
implementation is project-authored; its name dictionary is based on this
project's original MIT catalog, with aliases and locally maintained additions.
Ship ID mappings cover Japanese and Pan-Asian ships. ARP collaboration names
were checked against the WG API's Japanese localization; existing official
Chinese names are retained for the other reviewed collaboration ships.

React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Electron, electron-updater, Node.js, SQLite, Radix UI, Lucide,
Zod and build/test dependencies retain their respective licenses. Their
license files are distributed with their packages.
