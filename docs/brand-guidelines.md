# ApeRadar Next brand guidance

ApeRadar Next is an independent, local-first battle intelligence companion. Its
voice is calm and precise, with readable numbers and clear recovery actions.
Synthetic examples are always marked. Statistics describe the roster and do not
predict a battle's outcome.

Chinese is the default application language. Labels use the project terminology:
精英、熟练、标准、海猴、战绩隐藏、查询失败. Hidden profiles and unavailable requests
have distinct meanings. Messages explain the data state and the next useful action.

Visual identity follows the selected light or dark palette. Navigation, actions,
and the in-app mark share the active semantic tokens; team and rating colors keep
their meanings across palettes. Text and series styles supplement color.

The [design system](../design-system/aperadar-next/MASTER.md) owns the token, icon,
typography, component, and interaction rules. Asset provenance and licenses are
recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).
